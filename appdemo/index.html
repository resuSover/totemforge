Update firmware

  1. Keep the base plugged in and restart it in update mode. A drive named RPI-RP2 appears.

  2. Save the firmware file onto the RPI-RP2 drive. The base installs it and restarts by itself, and the drive disappears.

  3. Click Connect above to check that the base answers.

Don't unplug the base while the firmware is being saved. A warning that the drive was not ejected properly, or that the save could not finish, is normal: the base restarts as soon as it has the firmware.
