Firmware update

Connect to check whether your base needs an update.

  1. Restart the base in update mode. A drive named RPI-RP2 appears.

  2. Save the firmware onto RPI-RP2. The base installs it and restarts.

  3. Click Connect to check the new version.

Don't unplug the base while saving. A warning that the drive wasn't ejected properly is normal.
