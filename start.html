QUICK START GUIDE

1

Unbox your set

Open the base by pressing firmly on its top and bottom at the same time, then twist the lid to unscrew it.

Every Totem Forge set includes:

  • Your totems
  • The base, also your summoning circle and protective storage
  • A USB-C cable
  • An NFC tag linking to this guide (hidden in the center of the lid)
2

Install the app

Download the free Totem Forge companion app from Google Play. Android only for now.

GET IT ON GOOGLE PLAY
3

Add art to your gallery

  • Share any image from your browser or other apps straight to the Totem Forge app.
  • Or tap "+" and press Import Images to get it from your phone's gallery. Crop it and add tags and text afterwards.
  • Or tap "+" and press Import Art Packs for our hand-crafted packs: ready to use, with images, tags, and text already done.
4

Dock → Summon → Play

  1. Place a totem on the dock base by firmly pressing on it (check connector position first).
  2. Connect the base to your phone with the USB-C cable.
  3. The app opens automatically. Pick a portrait from your gallery or a curated art pack, and transfer it to the totem.
  4. Lift the totem off the base and set it on the table.

The e-ink screen holds the portrait without any power, so it stays visible all session, no phone required nearby.

5

Light it up (optional)

Press down on the colored insert to switch on the internal LED. Use it to signal status effects like poisoned or blessed, or just to make a boss moment glow.

The light runs on a small CR927 battery that lasts several months if turned off between sessions. To replace it: firmly press on the bottom piece of the totem from the side where the inserts are, then remove the insert and take out the LED unit to swap the battery.

6

Swap for next session

Dock the totem again anytime to load a different character. One set covers an entire campaign, one portrait at a time.
