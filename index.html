05 · FAQ

Does it need charging?
Not really. The screen itself needs no battery: e-ink holds the image physically, so the portrait stays forever without power. A small replaceable CR927 battery powers only the LED light effects, and lasts several months for normal use.
Does the screen really stay on all the time?
Yes. That's the magic of e-ink: the image is physically held by the display, so it's always visible. No backlight, no sleep mode, no power used.
Which games does it work with?
Any tabletop that uses tokens or miniatures. The 1-inch square footprint matches D&D and Pathfinder grids and blends with standard minis, so you can mix collections freely.
Where does the art come from?
Anywhere you like: import commissioned art or your own drawings, share any image straight to the app, or use our ready-made art packs tuned for e-ink.
Do I need the app to play?
Only to change what's on the screen: dock the totem on its base and connect to your phone via USB. During play the totem is fully standalone, and the portrait stays on without a phone nearby.
How is it shipped?
Each totem is assembled and tested in our workshop, then shipped tracked and padded. See the listing for current shipping regions and times.
