PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: 12 October 2026

In short

We don't collect your personal data. There are no accounts, no tracking, no cookies and no ads. Your images stay on your own device.

The Totem Forge app

  • Your images, tags and texts are stored only on your phone, tablet or computer. The app doesn't upload them anywhere.
  • The app talks to the Totem Forge base over its USB cable. On Android it asks for permission to use the base; in a browser it asks for access to MIDI devices, which is how it talks to the base.
  • The Android app has no internet access. The web app at totemforge.nl only loads its own files from our website and sends nothing back.
  • When you choose a photo, your device's photo picker shares only that photo with the app.

The totems and the base

They store no personal data. A totem only holds the picture you last put on it.

This website

  • No analytics, no cookies, no tracking. The fonts are served from our own website.
  • The website is hosted by GitHub Pages. To deliver the pages and keep them secure, GitHub processes technical data such as your IP address (see the GitHub privacy statement).
  • Links to Etsy, Google Play, Discord and other sites take you to services with their own privacy policies. Orders are handled by Etsy.

Email

If you email us at info@totemforge.nl, we use your message and address only to answer you, and delete them when they're no longer needed.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask what data we have about you, and have it corrected or deleted: write to info@totemforge.nl. You can also complain to the Dutch data protection authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

Who we are

Totem Forge is a project by resuSover, the Netherlands (KvK 42061826). We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the latest version.
